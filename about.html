About Chicago Pug Rescue

We're a community of pug lovers, advocates, and volunteers working to give every flat-faced dog a second chance in the Windy City.

Our Mission

Chicago Pug Rescue exists to educate Chicago-area residents about adopting and fostering pugs, and to connect them with the incredible local rescue organizations doing life-saving work every day.

We are not a shelter or rescue ourselves — we are an informational resource and community hub. We believe that an informed adopter is a prepared adopter, and that every pug deserves a home equipped to meet their unique needs.

Founded by a group of Chicago pug owners who experienced firsthand how confusing and overwhelming the rescue process can be, we built this site to make it easier for everyone — from first-time dog owners to experienced pet parents — to find their perfect wrinkled companion.

Our Story

📅

How It Started

In 2019, a group of pug owners connected through a Lincoln Square dog park started sharing resources about local rescues. What began as a group chat became a full-fledged website by 2021.

🌱

Growing the Community

We've helped connect thousands of Chicago families with rescue organizations, fosters, and veterinary resources. Our annual Pug Parade in Wicker Park draws hundreds of flat-faced fans.

🤝

Rescue Partnerships

We've partnered with over a dozen rescue organizations across Chicagoland to ensure our information is accurate, up-to-date, and truly useful for prospective adopters and fosters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you a rescue or shelter?

No. Chicago Pug Rescue is an informational website and community resource. We connect people with established rescue organizations — we do not house, adopt out, or care for animals directly.

How do you choose which rescues to list?

We manually vet every organization we list. We look for legitimate 501(c)(3) status, transparent adoption practices, positive community reputation, and genuine animal welfare focus.

Can I volunteer with Chicago Pug Rescue?

Absolutely! We are always looking for volunteers to help with content, events, social media, and community outreach. Reach out via our contact form and a team member will be in touch.

I found a stray pug in Chicago. What should I do?

If you find a stray pug, contact Chicago Animal Care and Control at (312) 744-5000. You can also post to local lost-pet Facebook groups and check with nearby vets to scan for a microchip.

Do you take donations?

We do not. We are fully volunteer-run with no operating costs. If you'd like to donate to support pugs in Chicago, we encourage you to give directly to any of our listed rescue partners.
